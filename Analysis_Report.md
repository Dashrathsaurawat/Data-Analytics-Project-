# 📄 Analysis Report  
**BigMart Customer Behavior Analysis**

## 🎯 Project Objective
To perform statistical analysis on BigMart customer data using SPSS and extract valuable business insights.

## 📚 Data Overview
- **Variables**: Age, Gender, Annual Income (k$), Spending Score (1-100), Product Category.
- **Sample Size**: 200 customers.

# 🔍 Detailed Analysis and Interpretations

## 1. Descriptive Statistics
- **Purpose**: Summarize key variables (Age, Income, Spending Score).
- **Insights**: 
  - Average Age = 36 years.
  - Average Income = $61k.
  - Average Spending Score = 50.

## 2. Frequency Distribution
- **Purpose**: Understand category distributions.
- **Insights**:
  - Majority are **Female** customers.
  - **Groceries** are the most purchased category.

## 3. Cross-tabulation (Gender vs Product Category)
- **Purpose**: See gender preference for product categories.
- **Insights**:
  - Females prefer **Groceries** and **Clothing**.
  - Males prefer **Electronics**.

## 4. Correlation Analysis (Income vs Spending Score)
- **Purpose**: Check relation between income and spending.
- **Insights**:
  - No strong correlation found. (Income doesn't predict spending behavior).

## 5. Independent Samples T-Test (Gender and Spending Score)
- **Purpose**: Compare male vs female spending.
- **Insights**:
  - **Females** spend significantly more.

## 6. ANOVA (Spending Score across Product Categories)
- **Purpose**: Compare spending among different categories.
- **Insights**:
  - Spending significantly varies among categories.

## 7. K-Means Clustering
- **Purpose**: Segment customers.
- **Insights**:
  - 3 clear customer segments based on age, income, and spending score.

## 8. Visualizations
- **Purpose**: Make interpretation easier.
- **Insights**:
  - Younger customers (low income) often spend more.

# 📈 Key Insights
- Focus on female shoppers.
- Promote Grocery and Clothing sales.
- Customize marketing based on clusters.

# 🏆 Conclusion
SPSS analysis provided BigMart actionable customer insights and better business strategies.
